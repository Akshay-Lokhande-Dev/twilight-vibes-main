Portland, Oregon uses the America/Los_Angeles time zone. The local clock changes between Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) and Pacific Standard Time (PST) depending on daylight saving time.

PDT vs PST in Portland

PDT means Pacific Daylight Time and corresponds to UTC−7. PST means Pacific Standard Time and corresponds to UTC−8. Which abbreviation applies depends on the date and daylight-saving rules in effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time zone is Portland, Oregon in?

Portland is in the America/Los_Angeles time zone, commonly known as Pacific Time.

Is Portland on PDT or PST?

It depends on the date. Portland switches between Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) and Pacific Standard Time (PST).

What is the UTC offset for Portland?

Portland uses UTC−7 during PDT and UTC−8 during PST.
