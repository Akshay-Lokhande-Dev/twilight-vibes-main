What Is Verity About?

Struggling writer Lowen Ashleigh (Dakota Johnson) is hired by Jeremy Crawford (Josh Hartnett) to finish the remaining books in a bestselling series written by his wife, Verity Crawford (Anne Hathaway) — who's been unable to write since a mysterious accident left her unable to walk or talk.

Lowen moves into the isolated Crawford estate to go through Verity's notes, and finds something she was never supposed to see: an unfinished, deeply disturbing manuscript that reads less like a novel and more like a confession. From there, the line between fiction and reality gets messier — and so does Lowen's relationship with Jeremy.

No further plot details here on purpose — if you haven't read the book, this is genuinely one you want to go in blind for.

Is Verity Based on a True Story?

No, it's fiction. Verity is a novel Colleen Hoover self-published in 2018, before Grand Central Publishing picked it up in 2021. It's not based on real people or events — it just reads like it could be, which honestly might be worse.

Is Verity Appropriate for Teens?

Verity is rated R. This isn't a Twilight-style YA romance — it's a genuinely dark, adult psychological thriller with disturbing content. If you're used to the Walter Boys or Summer I Turned Pretty level of intensity, this one's a different category entirely. Go in knowing that.

Why Everyone's Talking About It

Part of it is just Colleen Hoover's track record at this point — Verity is her fourth book to get the movie treatment after It Ends With Us, Regretting You, and Reminders of Him, and her fanbase shows up in force every single time.

But a lot of it is genuinely the trailer. "This place is insane, I feel like I'm losing my mind" lives in my head rent-free since I watched it. If the movie delivers even half of what that trailer promises, October is going to be a lot.
