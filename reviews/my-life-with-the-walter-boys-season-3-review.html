My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 Review

Is My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 worth watching? For fans of the first two seasons, yes. Season 3 keeps the messy romance that made the show addictive, but it also gives the Walter family more room to feel like an actual family instead of a backdrop for the love triangle.

Okay, so real talk — I wasn't even planning to watch this one immediately. But once I started, the season moved fast. Jackie, Cole, and Alex still have plenty of emotional baggage, but Season 3 feels more interested in what happens when these characters actually have to grow up and make choices.

Quick answer: Watch it if you enjoy teen romance, family drama, and cliffhanger-heavy finales. The ending is intentionally unresolved, so go in knowing Season 3 is not giving you a neat bow.

What Works in Season 3

What works
  • The romance has real consequences.
  • The Walter family dynamics get more breathing room.
  • Jackie feels more confident and active in her own story.
  • The finale gives the season a strong hook for Season 4.
What doesn't
  • Some emotional beats still feel stretched.
  • The cliffhanger may frustrate viewers who want closure.
  • The love triangle remains a major driver of the story.

Season 3 Ending Explained (FULL SPOILERS)

⚠️ Spoiler alert: Everything below discusses the Season 3 finale, including Cole, Jackie, Eliot, and the final car accident.

The finale deliberately leaves two major questions hanging. Jackie shares a kiss with Eliot, her childhood friend from New York, at the community center opening. At almost the same time, Cole — who has realized that breaking up with Jackie was a mistake — heads out to try to win her back and is involved in a serious car accident.

That means the season does not give Jackie a final romantic choice. Instead, it creates a new triangle around her while putting Cole's future in question.

Who Does Jackie Choose: Cole, Alex, or Eliot?

No one officially, by the end of Season 3. Jackie and Cole reconnect earlier in the season, but Cole breaks up with her before the finale. Alex has moved on with Kiley, while Eliot suddenly becomes a real romantic possibility after the final kiss.

So the most accurate answer is: Season 3 leaves Jackie’s love life unresolved. That is clearly setting up the emotional stakes for Season 4.

What We Know About Season 4

Netflix has confirmed Season 4, and production has already started in Calgary. Netflix says the fourth season is slated for a 2027 release, although an exact premiere date has not been announced.

That makes the Season 3 cliffhanger especially important: the next season is expected to address Cole's condition and Jackie’s reaction to Eliot's kiss.

How Many Episodes Are in Season 3?

Season 3 has 10 episodes. Netflix's official title page lists episodes through Episode 10, “Happily Ever After,” and Season 3 was released on August 6, 2026.

Final Verdict: Watch It or Skip It?

Watch it.

Especially if you've already seen Seasons 1 and 2 — you're not skipping this one. And if you're new to the show and like the messy, will-they-won't-they energy of Twilight's love triangle or The Summer I Turned Pretty, this is an easy recommendation.

Is My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 worth watching? Yes. The family drama is stronger, the characters have more room to grow, and the ending gives Season 4 plenty to work with. Just be ready for the cliffhanger.
