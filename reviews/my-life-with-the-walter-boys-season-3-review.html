My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 Review

Is My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 actually worth watching? If you've been invested since Season 1, the short answer is yes. Season 3 keeps the messy, high-stakes romance that made the show so addictive, but finally gives the Walter family more room to breathe.

Real talk — I wasn't even planning to binge this all in one night. But once Episode 1 kicked off, the pacing grabbed me. If you thought the Bella-Edward-Jacob triangle was stressful back in the day, Jackie’s situation this season is giving it a serious run for its money.

Jackie, Cole, and Alex still carry plenty of emotional baggage. However, Season 3 feels much more interested in what happens when these characters are actually forced to grow up and face the consequences of their choices.

Quick answer: Watch it if you enjoy intense YA romance, family drama, and chaotic finales. Just go in knowing that the ending is intentionally unresolved — Netflix is absolutely playing with our hearts here.

What Works in Season 3

What works
  • The romance finally has real-world consequences.
  • The Walter family dynamics get way more screen time.
  • Jackie feels confident, actively driving her own story.
  • The finale sets up a massive hook for Season 4.
What doesn't
  • Some emotional beats still feel a bit stretched out.
  • The dual cliffhanger will heavily frustrate viewers who want closure.
  • The love triangle is still the loudest part of the plot.

Season 3 Ending Explained: The Crash (FULL SPOILERS)

⚠️ Spoiler alert: Everything below discusses the major events of the Season 3 finale, including Cole's accident and Jackie's unexpected kiss. Read at your own risk!

If you're still processing that finale, you aren't alone. The writers deliberately left two massive, heart-stopping questions hanging right before the credits rolled.

First, Jackie shares a surprisingly passionate kiss with Eliot (her childhood friend from New York) at the community center opening. It throws a complete wrench into the dynamics we've gotten used to over the past two seasons.

At almost the exact same time, Cole realizes that breaking up with Jackie was the biggest mistake he's ever made. He gets in his car to win her back, but tragedy strikes. He is involved in a severe car accident, and the screen cuts to black before we find out if he's okay.

Who Does Jackie Choose: Cole, Alex, or Eliot?

The frustrating truth? No one officially. At least, not by the end of Season 3.

Jackie and Cole *do* reconnect earlier in the season (giving Team Cole fans plenty to cheer for), but Cole breaks up with her right before the finale. Meanwhile, Alex has completely moved on and is navigating a new dynamic with Kiley.

Just when you think Jackie might figure herself out, Eliot steps up as a very real romantic possibility. Season 3 leaves Jackie’s love life completely unresolved, perfectly setting the stage for an explosive Season 4.

Does Richard Get Custody of Jackie?

No — and that's the whole point. Season 3 opens with George having a stroke, which shakes the entire Walter house and sets the tone for a quieter, more careful season. Against that backdrop, Jackie's uncle Richard spends most of Season 3 being overbearing about her safety — grounding her, distrusting Cole, acting more like a warden than family.

The reason finally comes out around episode 6: Richard admits he wasn't actually given custody of Jackie when her parents died. That old rejection is what's been driving his need to prove he's "the responsible one" ever since. It reframes his whole arc — he's not really parenting Jackie, he's still relitigating a decision made about him years ago.

So no, Richard was never given custody, then or now — but understanding why makes him a lot easier to sit with by the end of the season.

What We Know About Season 4

Breathe easy—Netflix has officially confirmed Season 4. Even better, production has already started up in Calgary.

While an exact premiere date hasn't been locked in yet, Netflix has slated the fourth season for a 2027 release. Given the massive cliffhanger, Season 4 will have to immediately address Cole's medical condition and the fallout from Jackie and Eliot's kiss.

Final Verdict: Watch It or Skip It?

Watch it.

Especially if you've already survived Seasons 1 and 2 — you absolutely cannot skip this one. If you're new to the show but love the messy, will-they-won't-they energy of Twilight's love triangle or The Summer I Turned Pretty, this is an incredibly easy recommendation.

The family drama hits harder, the characters have matured, and the ending guarantees you'll be counting down the days until 2027.

Thanks again to everyone in the fandom for hyping this season up — turns out, the hype was totally earned.

FAQ: My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3

Is My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3 worth watching?

Yes, especially if you've seen Seasons 1 and 2. It's a step up in family drama and character development, although the finale ends on an unresolved cliffhanger.

What happens at the end of My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3?

Jackie kisses Eliot at the community center opening while Cole gets into a serious car accident after deciding he wants to win Jackie back. The finale leaves both storylines unresolved.

Who does Jackie choose in Season 3 — Cole, Alex or Eliot?

Neither officially. Cole and Jackie reconnect earlier in the season, Alex moves on with Kiley, and Eliot becomes a new romantic complication after the finale kiss.

Is there a Season 4 of My Life with the Walter Boys?

Yes. Netflix has confirmed Season 4, which is already in production and is slated for release in 2027.

Does Jackie end up with Cole or Alex in Season 3?

Neither officially. Cole and Jackie break up before the finale, while Alex moves on with Kiley. The season ends unresolved.

Who is Eliot in My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3?

Eliot is Jackie's childhood friend from New York who returns in Season 3 and becomes a romantic complication by the finale.

Does Richard get custody of Jackie in Season 3?

No. Season 3 reveals that Richard was never actually given custody of Jackie when her parents died — a secret that explains why he's been so overprotective of her all season.

What happens to George in My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3?

George has a stroke in the Season 3 premiere. He survives and recovers through the season, but it noticeably changes the mood in the Walter house.

Is My Life with the Walter Boys based on a book?

Yes. The Netflix series is based on Ali Novak's novel My Life with the Walter Boys.

How many episodes are in My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3?

There are 10 episodes in Season 3, and Netflix lists the season through Episode 10.

Who plays Jackie, Cole and Alex?

Nikki Rodriguez plays Jackie Howard, Noah LaLonde plays Cole Walter, and Ashby Gentry plays Alex Walter.

Does Cole die in My Life with the Walter Boys Season 3?

The season ends with Cole in a serious car accident, but the finale does not reveal his ultimate condition. Season 4 is expected to answer that cliffhanger.
