Okay so real talk, I started The Summer I Turned Pretty season 3 finale at like 11 pm thinking I will watch 20 minutes and sleep. Next thing I know its 1:40 am, my eyes red, and I am searching "flights to Paris" on Google. I am not even kidding, that finale messed me up.

If you been following Belly since season 1, you know how much she grown. Season 1 she was that shy girl, season 2 she was so confused, and season 3... season 3 she finally become a woman who know what she wants. And that journey is why this finale hits so hard.

I remember when I watched season 2, I was so team Jeremiah. Like I literally fought with my sister for him. She was team Conrad from day one. We didn't talked for 2 days because of that. So this season I came with full Jeremiah mindset. But man, Conrad in Paris... I changed my team in one scene.

Story So Far — Quick Recap (So You Don't Get Confused)

So season 3 start where season 2 ended. Belly got engaged to Jeremiah, which honestly I thought was so fast. Like girl you are 19, chill. Then whole summer at Cousins Beach is awkward, Conrad is distant, Jeremiah is trying to be perfect but you can see he is insecure. And then that fight in episode 5, when Jeremiah find that letter, that was point where I knew this engagement will not last.

Last 3 episodes they show Belly in Paris, trying to find herself. She doing internship, living alone, dating Benito for like 2 episodes. That Benito track was waste of time I felt, but okay they wanted to show she moved on. She didn't though.

Finale Episode 11 "At Last" — What Actually Happens (⚠️ SPOILERS)

⚠️ Spoiler warning — stop here if you haven't watched the finale yet.

Finale start in Paris. Its Belly's birthday, almost 22. She come back from bike ride with Benito, and guess who standing outside her apartment? Conrad. No text, no call, just standing there in that same old hoodie.

I swear my heart stopped for a second. He looks more mature now, little beard and all. He says "I was in London for work and... I couldn't not come."

They go for walk. He tells her he broke his engagement? No sorry not engagement, his thing with that other girl in California ended long back. He tells her he always had that infinity necklace she returned.

Then the main scene — at train station. Conrad is going back, his train is at 7:15. Belly is standing, she trying to be strong. Then she runs. Like full movie style running in Paris streets with that yellow dress. She reach station, train is leaving, and she shouts his name.

He gets down. And then she says that line — "In every universe, in every version of me, I would still choose you." I cried. I actually cried.

She show him she still have infinity necklace in her bag. He had kept her letter from 2 years ago.

Last 10 minutes is them coming back to Cousins Beach house together. No big wedding, no drama. Just them sitting on that beach porch, same place where season 1 started. Steven, Taylor, Laurel everyone there. Jeremiah also there, and surprisingly he smiles. He says "Took you guys long enough."

It ends with Belly voiceover — "This was the summer I finally became pretty... not because someone choose me, but because I choose myself first."

Who Does Belly End Up With? — For Everyone Searching This

Okay for all who just want answer — She ends up with CONRAD. Conrad Fisher is endgame. Just like books.

Jeremiah is okay, he gets closure, he will go to college and focus on his life. Show didn't made him villain which I liked. He is not bad guy, just not right for her.

And no, there is no season 4. Prime said this is final season. But there is rumour of movie, maybe next year. Jenny Han teased something about easter egg, so lets see.

My Honest Feelings — Is It Worth Watching?

What worked
  • The finale earns the ending — Conrad and Belly's chemistry finally feels real, not manufactured.
  • Belly's arc across 3 seasons pays off — she chooses herself before she chooses him.
  • No forced happy-ending vibe, it feels earned.
What didn't
  • Middle episodes 4, 5, 6 drag — same arguments on repeat.
  • The Benito storyline felt like filler.
  • Some dialogue feels a little too on-the-nose for real teenagers.

Look, I will not say its perfect. But finale? Finale makes up for everything. If you loved this show for 3 years, you owe yourself to watch this ending. Its emotional, its satisfying, and it feels real. Not like forced happy ending.

I watched Twilight and Walter Boys also, but TSITP always had more heart. Walter Boys is more spicy, Twilight is fantasy, but this one is like your own life story.

FAQ: The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 Finale

Who does Belly end up with in The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3?

Belly ends up with Conrad Fisher, same as in the books. Jeremiah gets closure and the show doesn't turn him into a villain — he's just not the right fit for her.

Is there a Season 4 of The Summer I Turned Pretty?

No, Prime Video has confirmed Season 3 is the final season. There's talk of a possible movie, and Jenny Han has teased something, but nothing is confirmed yet.

Is The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 finale worth watching?

Yes. The middle episodes run a little slow, but the finale makes up for it — it's emotional, satisfying, and doesn't feel like a forced happy ending.

What happens in The Summer I Turned Pretty finale, episode 11 "At Last"?

Conrad shows up in Paris on Belly's birthday. After a train station chase scene, Belly tells him she'd still choose him in every version of herself, and the finale ends with everyone back together at the Cousins Beach house.
