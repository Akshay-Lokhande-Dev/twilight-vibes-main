Okay, so real talk: I started The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 finale at around 11 PM thinking I'd watch 20 minutes and go to sleep. Next thing I know, it's 1:40 AM, my eyes are red, and I am literally searching "flights to Paris" on Google. I'm not even kidding—that finale messed me up.

If you've been following Belly since Season 1, you know exactly how much she's grown. In Season 1 she was that shy girl, Season 2 she was completely confused, but by Season 3... she finally becomes a woman who knows what she wants. And that journey is exactly why this finale hits so hard.

I remember when I watched Season 2, I was firmly Team Jeremiah. I literally fought with my sister over him (she was Team Conrad from day one, and we didn't talk for two days because of it). So, I went into this season with a full Jeremiah mindset. But man, Conrad in Paris... I changed my team in one scene.

The Story So Far — Quick Recap

Season 3 picks up where Season 2 left off. Belly gets engaged to Jeremiah, which honestly felt incredibly fast. Like, girl, you are 19—chill! The whole summer at Cousins Beach is awkward; Conrad is distant, and Jeremiah is trying to be perfect, but you can see how insecure he really is. When they have that massive fight in Episode 5 after Jeremiah finds the letter, that was the exact moment I knew the engagement wasn't going to last.

The last three episodes show Belly in Paris, finally trying to find herself. She's doing an internship, living alone, and dating Benito for a couple of episodes. The Benito track felt like a bit of a waste of time, but I get it—they needed to show she was trying to move on. Spoiler alert: she didn't.

Finale Episode 11 "At Last" — What Actually Happens (⚠️ SPOILERS)

⚠️ Spoiler warning: Stop reading here if you haven't watched the finale yet!

The finale kicks off in Paris. It's Belly's birthday—she's turning 22. She comes back from a bike ride with Benito, and guess who is standing outside her apartment? Conrad. No text, no call, just standing there in that same old hoodie.

I swear my heart stopped for a second. He looks so much more mature now, sporting a little beard. He says, "I was in London for work and... I couldn't not come."

They go for a walk, and he drops the bomb: he ended his thing with that girl in California a long time ago. Then, he reveals he always kept that infinity necklace she returned to him.

Then comes the main scene at the train station. Conrad is heading back, catching a 7:15 train. Belly is standing there, trying to be strong. And then... she runs. We get a full movie-style running scene through the Paris streets in that iconic yellow dress. She reaches the station as the train is leaving and screams his name.

He gets off the train. And then she delivers the line of the season: "In every universe, in every version of me, I would still choose you." I cried. I actually cried.

She shows him that she still has the infinity necklace in her bag, and he reveals he kept her letter from two years ago.

The last 10 minutes feature them coming back to the Cousins Beach house together. No big wedding, no unnecessary drama. Just them sitting on that beach porch, the exact same place where Season 1 started. Steven, Taylor, Laurel—everyone is there. Jeremiah is there too, and surprisingly, he smiles. He just says, "Took you guys long enough."

It all ends with Belly's voiceover: "This was the summer I finally became pretty... not because someone chose me, but because I chose myself first."

Who Does Belly End Up With?

Okay, for everyone desperately searching for this answer before watching: She ends up with CONRAD. Conrad Fisher is endgame, just like in the books.

Jeremiah gets proper closure. He's going to college and focusing on his own life. The show didn't turn him into a villain, which I really appreciated. He isn't a bad guy; he's just not the right guy for her.

And for those wondering, there is no Season 4. Prime Video stated this is the final season. However, there are rumors of a possible movie next year, and Jenny Han teased a little easter egg, so we will have to wait and see!

Is It Worth Watching?

What worked
  • The finale earns the ending — Conrad and Belly's chemistry finally feels real, not manufactured.
  • Belly's arc across 3 seasons pays off beautifully; she chooses herself before choosing him.
  • No forced happy-ending vibe—it feels genuinely earned.
What didn't
  • Middle episodes (4, 5, 6) drag slightly, with the same arguments on repeat.
  • The Benito storyline felt like filler.
  • Some dialogue still feels a little too on-the-nose.

Look, I won't say the season is perfect. But the finale? The finale makes up for everything. If you've loved this show for three years, you owe it to yourself to watch this ending. It's emotional, satisfying, and deeply real.

I've watched Twilight and Walter Boys, but TSITP always had more heart. Walter Boys is spicier, Twilight is pure fantasy, but this show feels like a piece of your own life story.

FAQ: The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 Finale

Who does Belly end up with in The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3?

Belly ends up with Conrad Fisher, exactly like in the books. Jeremiah gets solid closure and the show doesn't turn him into a villain — he's simply not the right fit for her.

Is there a Season 4 of The Summer I Turned Pretty?

No, Prime Video has confirmed Season 3 is the final season. There is talk of a possible movie, and Jenny Han has teased something, but nothing is officially confirmed yet.

Is The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 finale worth watching?

Yes. While the middle episodes run a little slow, the finale absolutely makes up for it. It's emotional, highly satisfying, and doesn't feel like a forced happy ending.

What happens in The Summer I Turned Pretty finale, episode 11 "At Last"?

Conrad shows up in Paris for Belly's birthday. After a dramatic train station chase scene, Belly tells him she'd still choose him in every version of herself. The series ends with everyone back together at the Cousins Beach house.
