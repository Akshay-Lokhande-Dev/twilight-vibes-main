Okay so the trailer for this just dropped and I have questions. Not "is this good" questions — genuine, structural questions, like: how did the director of Old and Knock at the Cabin end up making what looks like the most devastating Nicholas Sparks movie ever made? Nobody saw this pairing coming, and that's exactly why it's the preview everyone's been forwarding to their group chats this week.

If your algorithm has been feeding you the teaser on repeat too, here's everything actually confirmed so far, minus the speculation.

What Is Remain About?

Jake Gyllenhaal plays Tate Gordon, a reclusive New York architect who's recently been discharged from a psychiatric facility after being treated for depression, and is still grieving the death of his sister. Looking for a fresh start, he takes a job designing a summer home in a small coastal town in Cape Cod.

There, he meets Wren (Phoebe Dynevor) — described in the official synopsis as pulling him "out of his shell, and into the center of a deadly mystery that hangs over the town." The trailer leans hard into romance first: the two are shown playing board games, falling for each other, clearly happy. Then it turns. At one point Wren asks Tate, "Why can't we be together?" and he answers, quietly, "I think you know." Something is keeping them apart, and the trailer never quite says what.

No further plot details here on purpose — the whole appeal of a Shyamalan project is going in without knowing the twist, and this preview isn't going to be the thing that spoils it for you.

Release Date & Where to Watch

Remain opens internationally on February 3, 2027, followed by a North American theatrical release on February 5, 2027. This is a theatrical release timed for the Valentine's Day corridor, so expect a proper opening-weekend push rather than a quiet streaming drop.

It almost released last Halloween: Remain was originally scheduled for October 23, 2026, but Warner Bros. moved it to February to lean into the romance side of the marketing instead — reportedly after the film tested as one of the highest-scoring projects of Shyamalan's entire career.

Is Remain Based on a True Story?

No, it's fiction — but it's an unusual kind of fiction. Remain is an original story, not a real event, built from the ground up by Shyamalan and Sparks together before either of them wrote a single page.

Wait, Is This Based on a Book?

Sort of, but not in the usual way. Shyamalan and Sparks came up with the story together, then each went off and wrote their own version in their own medium — Sparks turned it into a novel (published October 2025), while Shyamalan independently wrote the film's screenplay. So the book and the movie share a premise and characters, but they aren't a straight adaptation of one another; they're two separate tellings of the same idea, finished around the same time.

Why Everyone's Talking About It

Mostly because nobody expected these two names in the same sentence. Sparks is the guy behind The Notebook and A Walk to Remember. Shyamalan is the guy behind twist endings that make you rewatch the whole movie immediately. Putting them in a room together to build a "supernatural love story" is such an unlikely combination that the trailer alone became a talking point the day it dropped.

Sparks has said in interviews that he and Gyllenhaal connected quickly once he came on board, and that Dynevor was one of a small shortlist considered for Wren. Combine that with reports that this tested as Shyamalan's best-received film yet, and it's easy to see why Warner Bros. shifted the release into the biggest date on the romance calendar instead of burying it in the Halloween slate.

FAQ: Remain (2027)

When does Remain come out?

Remain releases internationally on February 3, 2027, followed by a North American theatrical release on February 5, 2027.

Who stars in Remain?

Jake Gyllenhaal stars as Tate Gordon alongside Phoebe Dynevor as Wren, with Ashley Walters, Julie Hagerty, and Jay O. Sanders in supporting roles.

Is Remain based on a true story?

No. Remain is a fictional supernatural romance created by M. Night Shyamalan and Nicholas Sparks, who developed the same story as both a film and a novel.

Is Remain based on a book?

It's a parallel adaptation rather than a book-to-film adaptation. Shyamalan and Sparks developed the same original story together, with Sparks writing it as a novel (published October 2025) while Shyamalan wrote his own screenplay for the film.

Why was Remain's release date changed?

Remain was originally set for a Halloween 2026 release (October 23) but Warner Bros. moved it to the Valentine's Day corridor in February 2027, reportedly after strong test screening scores for the romance elements of the film.

Is Remain a horror movie?

Not exactly. Despite Shyamalan's horror-thriller reputation, Remain is being marketed as a supernatural romantic drama with mystery elements, closer to a Nicholas Sparks love story with an unsettling twist than a straight horror film.
