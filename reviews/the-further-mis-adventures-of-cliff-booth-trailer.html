The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth Trailer Is Here

Cliff Booth is back. Netflix has released the first full official trailer for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, and Brad Pitt is once again playing the character he first brought to life in Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood.

This time, though, things look very different. The story moves from 1969 to 1977, and Cliff is no longer mainly working as a stuntman. He is now working as a Hollywood fixer, taking on problems that other people would rather keep quiet.

That alone makes this movie feel different from the first one. Add David Fincher as director and Quentin Tarantino as writer, and there is a lot to talk about before the movie even arrives.

What Is The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth About?

The movie takes place eight years after the events of Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood. Cliff is living in a changing Los Angeles and picking up different kinds of work.

When he is not working as a fixer, the story says he also earns money as a bartender and a projectionist. His fixer work is where things get interesting. He gets a call from talent agent Karen Crosby, played by Elizabeth Debicki, who wants his help with her client, TV star Charlie Peters, played by Scott Caan.

Charlie is known for doing his own stunts, which is a nice link to Cliff's old life. But Charlie's world is not as simple as it looks. His wife Toni, played by Carla Gugino, and former football champion Roderick “Hot Rod” Harrison, played by Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, are also part of the story.

Then there are the Verdusco brothers, played by Matt Groove and JB Tadena. They are gangsters causing trouble around Los Angeles.

The simple version: Cliff gets a job helping people in Hollywood, but the job pulls him into a much bigger problem involving crime, secrets and people who are not easy to deal with.

Is The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth a Movie or Series?

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is a movie, not a TV series. Netflix lists it as a film starring Brad Pitt, with David Fincher directing and Quentin Tarantino writing the screenplay. It will have a two-week IMAX theatrical run before its Netflix release.

The Adventures of Cliff Booth: The Earlier Title

If you have been searching for The Adventures of Cliff Booth, you are looking for the same Cliff Booth project. That was the title used earlier in the movie's development; the official release title is now The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.

The name The Adventures of Cliff Booth is also being used for Quentin Tarantino's upcoming Cliff Booth novel, which is scheduled for December 8, 2026.

What Happened to Cliff Booth?

If you remember the first movie, Cliff was mostly known as Rick Dalton's stunt double, driver and close friend. He was also much tougher than he first appeared.

Now the story has moved forward. Cliff is on his own, and his skills have become his job.

The trailer also gives us a small but important reminder of his past with Rick. Cliff explains that Rick got too heavy for him to keep doubling for him. That simple detail tells us why their old working relationship is no longer the center of Cliff's life.

Instead, Cliff has found a different place in Hollywood. He can deal with people, he can handle dangerous situations, and he is not scared of getting his hands dirty when a job requires it.

That feels like a natural next step for the character. We already knew Cliff could get himself out of trouble. Now we get to see what happens when getting people out of trouble becomes his actual job.

David Fincher and Quentin Tarantino Are Working Together

This is probably the biggest reason the new movie feels so different.

Tarantino wrote the screenplay, but David Fincher is directing. Tarantino created Cliff Booth in the original movie, so the character still comes from the same writer. But Fincher gets to decide how this new story looks and feels.

Brad Pitt and Fincher have already worked together on Se7en, Fight Club and The Curious Case of Benjamin Button. This movie brings them together again, but in a very different kind of story.

The trailer does show a darker side of Los Angeles. There are more night scenes, more crime and a stronger feeling that Cliff is getting pulled into something he may not be able to simply walk away from.

At the same time, the dialogue still has the relaxed feel you would expect from a Tarantino script. The two styles are not identical, and that is what makes the combination worth watching.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth Cast

Brad Pitt returns as Cliff Booth. Around him is a new group of characters who bring Cliff into this new part of Hollywood.

Elizabeth Debicki as Karen Crosby

Debicki plays Karen Crosby, a talent agent who calls Cliff for help with her client Charlie Peters.

Scott Caan as Charlie Peters

Caan plays Charlie Peters, a TV star who is known for doing his own stunts. His problems are what bring Cliff into the main story.

Carla Gugino as Toni

Gugino plays Toni, Charlie's wife. She is part of the group around Charlie that Cliff has to deal with.

Yahya Abdul-Mateen II as Roderick “Hot Rod” Harrison

Abdul-Mateen II plays a former football champion who has become a screen star.

The Verdusco Brothers

Matt Groove and JB Tadena play the Verdusco brothers, two gangsters who add another problem to Cliff's already messy job.

The wider cast also includes Peter Weller, Corey Fogelmanis and Karren Karagulian.

Is The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth a Sequel?

It continues the story of Cliff Booth from Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood, but it is not a traditional sequel where the whole original cast comes back for another Rick Dalton and Cliff Booth story.

The new movie is centered on Cliff. It takes place eight years later and gives him a completely different situation to deal with.

That means you do not need another version of the first movie to understand what this one is trying to do. But watching Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood first will obviously give you more context about Cliff, Rick and the world he came from.

And yes, the trailer makes it clear that Cliff's past with Rick still matters to the character.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth Release Date

The movie is getting a two-week exclusive IMAX run starting November 25, 2026.

After the IMAX release, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth will arrive on Netflix on December 23, 2026.

So if you want to see it on the biggest screen, the November release is the one to remember. If you would rather watch at home, you only need to wait until December.

There Is Also a Cliff Booth Novel

There is another interesting part of this release. Quentin Tarantino is also publishing a new book called The Adventures of Cliff Booth.

The book is scheduled to be published on December 8, 2026, which puts it between the movie's IMAX release and its Netflix release.

Tarantino previously turned Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood into a novel, so this gives fans another way to spend time with Cliff's character outside the movie.

Our Take From the Trailer

The main thing that stands out is that this movie does not look like it is trying to copy the first one.

The first film had a very different mood. It was built around 1969 Hollywood, Rick Dalton, Cliff's friendship with him and Tarantino's own version of that time in Los Angeles.

This movie moves forward to 1977. Cliff is older, he is working alone, and his job now puts him around people with much bigger problems.

That gives the story a good reason to exist. We are not simply watching Cliff repeat the same job again. We are seeing what happened to him after his old life changed.

The Fincher connection is also hard to ignore. The trailer has a darker look, but it still has the kind of character conversations that make the original movie feel like a Tarantino story.

We obviously cannot judge the full movie from one trailer. But there is enough here to make the new Cliff Booth story feel like its own thing rather than a simple copy of Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood.

And honestly, that is probably the best thing about it.
